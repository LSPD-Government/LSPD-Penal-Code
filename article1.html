Title 3 — Guidelines

P.C.GuidelineRule
1305Gathering PermitA permit may be required for gatherings over 5 people in public spaces, including gatherings by public entities.
1306AttemptPersons who attempt a crime may be guilty of the attempted crime.
1307Conspiracy — FelonyFelony conspiracy carries the full time of the felony that would have been committed; murder conspiracy is treated as first degree due to intent and deliberation.
1308Built-up Areas80 MPH.
1309Highway100 MPH.
1310RICO ActAllows criminal penalties and civil causes of action for acts performed as part of an ongoing criminal organization.
1311CourtContested charges may be brought to court immediately when an active judge is online and the defendant requests a hearing with or without a lawyer; otherwise P.C. 1315 may apply.
1312Time ServedApplies to time waiting for a lawyer, not normal processing unless officers are negligent.
1313Maximum PunishmentsUse maximum punishments only when the offense warrants them; punishment should match the offense level.
1314Bench TrialAnyone may request a bench trial and must serve the minimum required time while awaiting it; the request is filed through the court system.
1315Summary JudgmentsA law or peace officer may enact a summary judgment under the specified circumstances and must advise the defendant of contest procedures.
1318Heinous ClauseHeinous crimes may be denied a plea deal.
1319aWeapons License CostClass 1: $20,000 • Class 2: $60,000 • Class 3: $100,000.

1326Lawsuit CapIndividuals: $1,000,000. Agencies: $5,000,000.
1327CertificatesBusiness: $50,000 • Marriage: $50,000 • Liquor: $1,000,000.
