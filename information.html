INFORMATION

General information about the State of San Andreas Penal Codes and how to use this reference.

Purpose

This website provides a centralized reference for the State of San Andreas Penal Codes. It separates Article I — Civil Codes from Article II — Criminal Codes so users can quickly locate the rule or charge they need.

Article I

Article I contains Civil Codes, including sentencing definitions, legal definitions, weapon and substance classifications, government-related definitions, and state guidelines.

Article II

Article II contains the criminal codes used in the MDT. Each entry includes its P.C. number, offense name, classification, sentence, fine, and category. Use the search bar and filters to quickly locate a specific charge.

Learn the Codes

Important: Officers and department members should make an effort to learn and remember the commonly used Penal Codes instead of relying on the website for every interaction.

Use this site as a reference when you need to verify a P.C. number, classification, sentence, fine, or definition. Familiarity with the codes helps keep traffic stops, arrests, reports, and other department actions consistent.

Using Search

  • Search by P.C. number, such as 1001 or 8001.
  • Search by an offense name, such as Robbery or Evading.
  • On Article II, combine the search with category and classification filters.
  • The search bar in the header can be used from any page and will take you to the matching Article II results.
